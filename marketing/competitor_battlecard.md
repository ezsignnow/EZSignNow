## 5. Active Migration Assistance Guides

We make the transition from signNow completely painless. Use these three built-in migration pathways to convert hesitant prospects.

### 1. The Smart PDF Template Importer
* **How it works:** Clients can export their existing active templates from signNow as flat PDFs. 
* **The Technology:** When they upload these PDFs into ezsignnow, our proprietary layout engine automatically analyzes the document and pre-maps standard signature, date, and text fields where the old signNow fields were.
* **The Result:** Slashes template creation time by 90%, letting clients recreate their library in minutes.

### 2. Directory Bulk Import (CSV / JSON)
* **How it works:** To ensure users don’t lose their client databases, we offer a bulk-import utility.
* **The Process:** Clients export their address book/directory from signNow as a CSV.
* **The Upload:** They upload the CSV into the ezsignnow Settings panel. The system maps First Name, Last Name, Email, and Company, immediately provisioning their contact database so agents can auto-populate client details when sending documents.

### 3. White-Glove Defector Service (Dedicated Transition Program)
* **For High-Value Defectors:** Any company migrating from signNow with 10+ users is eligible for our White-Glove Service.
* **The Service:**
  * **Dedicated Onboarding Manager:** A real human assigned to their account to handle setup.
  * **Manual Template Re-creation:** Our team will manually recreate and format up to 50 complex signNow templates (including conditional logic and advanced form fields) completely free of charge.
  * **Parallel Run Period:** We offer up to 60 days of free service to allow a parallel run period, ensuring they don't pay for two systems while their old signNow contract expires.
