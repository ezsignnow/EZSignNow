## 🚀 How to Execute Your Kit Email Campaigns Successfully

To achieve the highest click-through rates (CTR) and conversion rates, follow these best practices when deploying these broadcasts in Kit:

1. **Leverage Kit's Native A/B Testing**:
   * When setting up your Broadcast in Kit, click the **A/B** icon next to the Subject Line input.
   * Input two of the provided high-CTR options. Kit will automatically send Option A to 15% of your list, Option B to 15%, track the winner after 4 hours based on open rates, and deliver the winning subject line to the remaining 70%.
2. **Personalization Tags**:
   * The templates use `{{ subscriber.first_name | default: "there" }}`. Ensure your subscriber fields are mapped correctly in Kit. If you don't collect first names, the default fallback ensures the copy reads naturally (e.g., "Dear there," or "Dear Professional,").
3. **UTM Link Tracking**:
   * Add UTM tags to the links in your emails to track conversions in Google Analytics/Supabase.
   * *Example*: `https://www.ezsignnow.com/?utm_source=kit&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=flat_pricing_disruption`
4. **Resend to Unopens**:
   * 48 hours after sending a broadcast, use Kit's **"Resend to Unopens"** feature. Change the subject line slightly (e.g., add "[Reminder]" or use the third subject line option provided in the suite) to capture an additional 8-15% open rate boost.
